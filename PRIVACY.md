# Saintiment — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026.

## Current stage

The project is in preparation. No LinkedIn research corpus has been collected, and no research collection service is operating. This policy explains project correspondence and the intended handling of data if collection proceeds. It does not claim LinkedIn approval or university affiliation.

Before research collection begins, we will publish the applicable study details: the responsible data controller and contact details, sources and data categories, purposes and legal basis, recipients and processing locations, retention, and any required participant information. This preparation-stage policy is not a substitute for those details or for required permissions.

## Information and purpose

If you contact us, we use the name, contact details, and message you provide to answer and manage your request. Where the GDPR applies, the basis for ordinary correspondence is our legitimate interest in responding to project inquiries; handling statutory rights requests also serves our legal obligations. Providing ordinary correspondence is voluntary, but we may be unable to respond without contact information.

Depending on the approved source and study, research data may include relevant posts or comments, dates, content identifiers, engagement information, and necessary professional context. Analysis may produce annotations, sentiment labels, and stakeholder categories. We will use only the fields necessary and permitted for the stated study. These are planned categories, not a claim that an API provides them.

Data may come from authorized LinkedIn access or other legitimately supplied research sources, as specified for each study. Public visibility alone is not permission to collect or reuse personal data. If account authorization is used, the requested information, collection frequency, uses, and withdrawal process will be explained before authorization. We do not ask for LinkedIn passwords.

Research methods may include human annotation and computational analysis. Model development, external AI services, and publication remain subject to the permissions for the particular data; this policy grants no additional rights. The research is not a service for advertising, sales prospecting, or employment decisions about individuals.

## Access, protection, and disclosure

Access to personal research data will be limited to authorized people and necessary service providers under applicable confidentiality, security, and data-use requirements. Before research processing, we will establish appropriate access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, and deletion procedures. We do not claim that a research storage system has already been deployed.

Personal research data and access credentials will not be committed to the public repository. Publications will use aggregate or otherwise suitably non-identifying findings where permitted. Removing names alone does not necessarily prevent identification. Any additional disclosure must have a lawful basis and comply with source permissions, including legally required disclosures.

This policy is hosted on GitHub. Visiting it is also subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); the project adds no tracking scripts to this document. This repository does not host personal research data. Correspondence is handled through the contact email service. Any research service providers, international transfers, and applicable safeguards will be identified before their use.

## Retention and deletion

We retain correspondence only while needed to resolve the inquiry and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. Research retention will follow the permitted purpose and the applicable source, legal, and institutional requirements. There is no default permanent archive. Shorter source-specific limits and deletion obligations apply to working copies, logs, caches, and backups; anonymization does not automatically remove those obligations.

## Your choices and rights

Contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, as applicable to your data and local law. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to the relevant data protection authority. We may request proportionate information to verify and locate your data, and will handle requests within applicable legal and platform deadlines. If a lawful exception applies, we will explain it.

Disconnecting an authorized LinkedIn application stops future authorized access; contact us separately about data already received. We cannot retrieve an individual's contribution from genuinely anonymous results. Updates will appear here with a revised date; material changes will be communicated and consent renewed where required.
