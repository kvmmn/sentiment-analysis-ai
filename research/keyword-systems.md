2. Two layers. Layer A = architecture AND GenAI. Layer B = skill/deskilling as a second filter.
3. Construction/AEC/built environment/sociotechnical-lifecycle strings stay related-work.
4. Do not cite 71 opinions / 6173 words as a published fact until a PDF is in hand. Exact dual match NOT identified in open full text (2026-08-31).

6. Closest object+sentiment paper, methods unread: Larbi et al. (2026), TFSC 228:124689, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2026.124689. Circumstantial 76% of 71 is not evidence.
7. Primary literature string is S-A1 / W-A1. No LIMIT-TO Engineering. No deskilling in A. Live counts not run.
8. Persian/German lists are separate. Explicit GAPs: ISO 6707 FA/DE; UNESCO FA; no official FA/DE hashtag sets; do not invent archviz calques; `مهارت‌زدایی` as Braverman equivalent (GAP — use English deskilling in mixed FA scholarly queries); `hushvare`/`humas` not approved. **`هوش مصنوعی مولد`:** sourced journal/CPD discourse (Honar-ha-ye Ziba; IRCEO LMS) — **not** Farhangestan-approved equivalent (see lexicon draft conflict below).
